# Privacy

Bevi is a small app for a group of friends to rank drinks. This note says what it keeps about you and why.

## What we store

- Your username and display name, so friends can find you.
- Your birth date, only to decide whether to show beer, wine and spirits (21 and over). Other people never see it.
- Your timezone, to count streak days.
- Your rankings, notes, Want to Try list, who you follow, and the comparisons you make while ranking.
- Photos you add of a Bev or a nutrition label.

## Where it lives

Everything is stored in our Supabase database and file storage. Your sign-in session is kept in this browser's local storage.

## Who can see it

People signed in to Bevi can see your display name, username, rankings and notes, who you follow, and how many Bevs you've saved to Want to Try. When you save a Bev, your friends see it in their feed. Your birth date is never shown to anyone else.

## What we don't do

- No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- We never sell or share your data.
- Location is never collected.

## Deleting your account

Settings › Delete account removes your profile, rankings, comparisons, follows and Want to Try. Bevs you added, and their photos, stay in the catalog without your name on them.

## Questions

Ask the person who invited you to Bevi.
