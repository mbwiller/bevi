# Privacy

Bevi is a small app for a group of friends to rank drinks. This note says what it keeps about you and why.

## What we store

- Your username and display name, so friends can find you.
- Your birth date, only to decide whether to show beer, wine and spirits (21 and over). Other people never see it.
- Your timezone, to count streak days.
- Your rankings, notes, Want to Try list, who you follow, and the comparisons you make while ranking.
- Photos you add of a Bev or a nutrition label.
- Where you got a drink, when you add one: the shop, cafe or bar you pick is saved with that ranking.

## Where it lives

Everything is stored in our Supabase database and file storage. Your sign-in session is kept on your device.

## Who can see it

People signed in to Bevi can see your display name, username, rankings and notes, who you follow, and how many Bevs you've saved to Want to Try. When you save a Bev, your friends see it in their feed, with the place you picked if you added one. Your birth date is never shown to anyone else.

## What we don't do

- No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- We never sell or share your data.
- Your phone's position is never stored. If you allow location, Add location uses it to list the places nearest you: it is sent with that one search and then dropped. When a search falls back to Google's directory, Google gets the search and a rounded position (to about 100 metres).

## Deleting your account

Settings › Delete account removes your profile, rankings, comparisons, follows, Want to Try and the places on your rankings. Bevs you added, and their photos, stay in the catalog without your name on them.

## Questions

Ask the person who invited you to Bevi.
